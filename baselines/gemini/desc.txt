This folder contains baselines based on prompting Gemini.


gemini_desc.py implements a two-stage prompting pipline where the first stage prompts Gemini to produce detailed, but general, caption of the video.
The second stage employs some LLM to evaluate the engagement level of a video based on its caption. An example for each label is provided, which forms 
three-shot prompting.


gemini_video.py directly prompts Gemini with input videos to predict the engagement. Noticeably, three-shot prompting with videos is not as effective as 
zero-shot prompting.
